# 7 Prompts to Uncover Hidden Career Opportunities

Upload your resume to Claude or ChatGPT to uncover positions you never realized matched your experience. Here are the 7 prompts to try:

---

## 1. Hidden Role Finder

> "Analyze my CV and identify 15 job titles that match my experience, skills, achievements, and transferable abilities. Include roles outside my current industry and rank them by fit, salary potential, demand, and entry difficulty."

* **Goal:** Discover 15 realistic career options in under 10 minutes instead of applying only to familiar positions.

---

## 2. Transferable Skills Translator

> "Review my CV and extract every transferable skill employers could value. Match each skill to suitable industries, departments, and job titles, then explain how my past experience proves I can perform those roles."

* **Goal:** Turn one work history into 20-30 marketable skills and unlock careers you may already qualify for.

---

## 3. Qualification Gap Analyzer

> "Compare my CV with this target role: [PASTE JOB DESCRIPTION]. Show which requirements I already meet, which are partially covered, and which are missing. Suggest the fastest way to close each important gap."

* **Goal:** Measure your fit across 10-15 requirements and build a focused improvement plan instead of rejecting yourself too early.

---

## 4. Career Pivot Mapper

> "Use my CV to create 5 realistic career-pivot paths. For each path, include the starting role, transferable experience, missing skills, recommended projects, likely salary range, and a 30-day transition plan."

* **Goal:** Convert one existing background into 5 practical career directions with clear first steps for the next month.

---

## 5. Best-Fit Industry Scanner

> "Analyze my experience and identify 10 industries where my skills could be valuable. Rank them by hiring demand, compensation, remote-work potential, career growth, and how easily I could enter without starting from zero."

* **Goal:** Find 10 new industries in under 15 minutes and expand your search beyond one overcrowded job market.

---

## 6. Resume Positioning Rewriter

> "Rewrite my professional summary and experience bullets for this new career direction: [TARGET ROLE]. Emphasize relevant achievements, transferable skills, measurable outcomes, and industry language without inventing qualifications."

* **Goal:** Transform one general CV into a targeted 1-2 page application aligned with the role's strongest requirements.

---

## 7. Opportunity Search Strategy

> "Based on my CV and target roles, create a 30-day job-search plan. Include suitable job titles, search keywords, company types, application targets, networking actions, recruiter outreach, and weekly performance checkpoints."

* **Goal:** Build a complete 30-day system covering 50-100 targeted applications and outreach messages instead of searching randomly.
